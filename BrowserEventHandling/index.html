Forbidden Relic

An ancient relic is protected by Loki's illusion

How to complete: choose Activate Relic, then right-click the relic image. The normal context menu is cancelled and a warning appears. You may try again freely

🌈 Illusion II

Bifrost Portal

The Bifrost demands consent before passage

How to complete: choose Open Portal, click Portal to Asgard, then answer the confirmation. OK opens marvel.com; Cancel keeps you in the Archive

mouseenter / coordinates III

🔮 Illusion III

Mischievous Artifact

A trickster artifact escapes the cursor

How to complete: choose Summon Artifact and move the pointer onto it. Every mouseenter sends it to new random top and left coordinates

delegation / closest() I

👑 Trial I

Council of Realms

Council records may be dismissed from one table

How to complete: choose Convene Council, then press Dismiss on any row. One table listener removes that row. Continue dismissing other members without Reset

delegation / computed style II

📖 Trial II

Archive of Illusions

Fifty magical runes alter the page aura

How to complete: choose Open Archive and click any rune. The page adopts its computed color. Select other runes as many times as you wish

bubbling / stopPropagation III

🔔 Trial III

Hall of Echoes

An echo crosses three nested chambers

How to complete: choose Enter Hall. Click Hidden Core once to log Hidden Core → Rune Chamber → Outer Hall. Click it again to stop propagation inside Hidden Core

CustomEvent / detail I

⚔️ Destiny I

The Sacred Relic

A relic shelf speaks to an independent Ancient Vault

How to complete: choose Reveal Relic, then press Claim Relic. Every click dispatches artifact-claimed and the independent Ancient Vault updates its count and power

keydown / buffer II

🔐 Destiny II

Secret Rune Sequence

A hidden identity waits behind four letters

How to complete: choose Begin Incantation and press L, O, K, I once each. Held-key repeats are ignored, so one physical press always fills one rune slot

click / Date.now() III

⏳ Destiny III

Temporal Echo

Two rapid touches awaken a dormant relic

How to complete: choose Reveal Echo and click the relic twice within 500 ms. Each valid pair awakens it; repeat the pair whenever you wish

mousemove / clientX,Y I

Emerald Familiar

A small familiar follows motion through the chamber

How to complete: choose Summon Familiar and move the cursor across the chamber. The orb follows clientX and clientY with a small smooth delay

keydown / event.key II

✒️ Interface II

Pure Rune Script

The Sacred Scroll accepts letters alone

How to complete: choose Open Scroll, type a few letters, then deliberately press a digit such as 7 or a symbol such as @. The forbidden key must not appear and the field turns red for one second

scroll / scrollTo III

⬆️ Interface III

Timeline Ascent

A sigil returns the Archive to its beginning

How to complete: choose Bind Ascent Sigil, scroll farther than one viewport, then use the fixed gold button to return smoothly to the top

keydown / style.top,left I

♟️ Motion I

Trickster's Path

Loki's sigil moves within a sealed arena

How to complete: the rune ᛚ is the hero — you do not type it. Choose Open Arena and use the physical keyboard arrows ← ↑ ↓ →. To run, hold Shift while pressing an arrow

mousedown / mousemove / mouseup II

🎚️ Motion II

Enchantment Resonance

A rune controls the strength of an enchantment

How to complete: choose Tune Enchantment. Press the thumb, drag it along the track, and release it. You can adjust it repeatedly without Reset

scroll / page metrics III

📜 Motion III

Endless Archive

New chronicles emerge near the end of the page

How to complete: choose Open Endless Archive and scroll within 100 px of the page bottom. One batch of five chronicles loads per approach; the safe demonstration limit is 40 records

native drag and drop I

🗂️ Craft I

Board of Fate

Destiny cards travel between three chambers

How to complete: choose Open Fate Board and drag any card into another column. Native dragstart, dragover and drop remain available for further moves

mouse geometry / intersection II

🖥️ Craft II

Illusion Selection

Hidden scrolls can be selected as on a desktop

How to complete: choose Open Illusion Desktop, press the dotted empty background and drag across scroll icons. This is selection, not Drag and Drop: the icons do not move. The rectangle disappears on release, while selected icons stay highlighted

scrollIntoView / viewport III

Timeline ScrollSpy

A fixed Fate menu observes three long sections

How to complete: choose Open Timeline Map. Use the fixed menu for smooth navigation or scroll manually. The visible section is highlighted; reach Hidden Future to complete
